Responsive variants (same photos, resized and WebP-encoded, no other changes):
  hero-1280.webp, hero-1600.webp, hero-2000.webp   <- hero.jpg
  hero-mobile-600.webp, hero-mobile-900.webp       <- hero-mobile.jpg
  story-800.webp, story-1200.webp, story-1600.webp <- story.jpg

Browsers load the WebP variants; the JPEGs are the fallback. Replace these files with the
store's own photography at any time: keep the file names and regenerate the WebP widths, or
adjust Views/Shared/Partials/Home.Hero.cshtml and Home.Story.cshtml.
